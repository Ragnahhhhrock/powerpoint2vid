Pricing questions

How much does the PowerPoint to video converter cost?

PowerPoint2Vid Pro is A$12 per month or A$99 per year. Both include unlimited slides, narration from speaker notes, captions and no watermark.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Use the manage link in your Stripe receipt email. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

Is my presentation uploaded when I pay?

No. Payment is handled by Stripe. Your slides are still parsed, narrated and recorded locally in your browser.

Do you offer team or bulk pricing?

Email contact@powerpoint2vid.com with how many people need access and we will sort something out.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Cards, Apple Pay, Link and Klarna, via Stripe's secure checkout.
