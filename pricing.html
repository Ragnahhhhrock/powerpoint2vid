Pricing questions

How much does the PowerPoint to video converter cost?

Basic needs no subscription; its exports carry a watermark. Pro is A$29 per month with 10 a.i.-directed explainer videos included, where a.i. tightens the story and redesigns each slide as an animated scene, and no watermark.

Can I buy one video without subscribing?

Yes. Pay per video is A$5 for one a.i.-directed explainer video with no watermark and no subscription. Purchased videos do not expire, and a video that fails to finish is given back. If you make more than five videos a month, Pro works out cheaper.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Open your account page and choose Manage subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

Does paying change how my slides are handled?

No. Payment is handled by Stripe, which never sees your slides. Your deck is used only to make your video.

Do you offer team or bulk pricing?

Email contact@powerpoint2vid.com with how many people need access and we will sort something out.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Cards, Apple Pay, Link and Klarna, via Stripe's secure checkout.
