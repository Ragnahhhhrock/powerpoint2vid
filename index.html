PowerPoint to video converter: common questions

What is the best online PowerPoint to video converter?

PowerPoint2Vid is a PowerPoint to video converter that runs in your browser. It adds voiceover from your speaker notes, animated reveals, transitions and captions, with no watermark.

How do I convert a PowerPoint to a video?

Drop your .pptx into the PowerPoint to video converter, choose a voice and video style, preview it, then export. Everything runs in your browser.

Can I convert Google Slides to video?

Yes. Download your deck from Google Slides as a PowerPoint (.pptx) file, then drop it in. The Google Slides tab gives you the export link.

What happens to my presentation?

Your deck is used only to make your video. It is not published or shared.

Where does the narration come from?

From your speaker notes, or slide text if there are no notes, using your browser’s built-in text-to-speech voices. Available voices depend on your device.

What video format does the PowerPoint to video converter export?

Your browser records the video, usually as WebM (MP4 in browsers that support it). WebM plays in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and most video editors, and can be converted to MP4 with most video tools.

Will it look exactly like my PowerPoint?

It renders text, shapes, images and backgrounds. Complex effects such as SmartArt, charts, custom animations and embedded video are simplified or skipped.
