# PowerPoint2Vid for Teachers: Go-to-Market Plan

26 Oct 2026 · Mal Gordon · Live version: https://claude.ai/artifact/6QddcznEEmf3VhobcmNmV9

## Summary

Teachers are the best-fit audience PowerPoint2Vid has: they already own hundreds of slide decks, and they are short of time. Australian full-time teachers report a median 50-hour week in term, with over 9 hours a week on lesson planning ([AITSL](https://qct.edu.au/news/2025/atwd-survey-open)). Australian lower secondary teachers work the third-longest week in the OECD ([TALIS 2024](https://www.pearlsandirritations.com/post/2025/11/oecd-survey-shows-australian-teachers-have-a-very-heavy-workload/)).

The pitch: **your existing slides become polished, narrated lesson videos you can reuse every year, so you teach once and the video covers the rest** (absences, revision, flipped lessons, catch-up).

Three moves:

1. **Win individual teachers first** with a Teacher plan priced below Pro, sold before Term 1 2027 while teachers plan over summer.
2. **Turn teachers into champions** who bring in their faculty, then their school, through a school licence and a 6-week pilot.
3. **Get procurement-ready for systems** (Department of Education WA, Catholic Education WA, AISWA, then other states) with an ST4S assessment, Australian data hosting and invoice billing.

The main threat is that teachers expect tools at no cost: Canva gives K-12 schools and teachers its education product at no charge ([Canva](https://www.canva.com/education/schools/)). PowerPoint2Vid wins on outcome, not price: a finished, a.i.-directed video, not a design tool.

## Teacher pain points and how PowerPoint2Vid helps

Every pain below traces back to one thing: teachers explain the same content many times, to students who are absent, behind, ahead or easily distracted.

| Pain point | What it looks like in a week | How PowerPoint2Vid helps | Feature to lead with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No time | Planning and prep spill into nights and weekends. | One upload turns an existing deck into a finished lesson video. No recording, editing or retakes. | Speaker notes become narration |
| Re-teaching for absent students | Catch-up packs, emails to parents, lunchtime re-explaining. | Post the lesson video in the LMS; absent students watch the same lesson. | MP4 download for any LMS |
| Mixed abilities in one class | Some students need it again; others want to move ahead. | Students pause and rewatch at their own pace; extension videos for fast finishers. | Reusable videos |
| Relief and sick days | Relief teachers deliver content they didn't plan. | Leave a narrated video so the lesson is still taught your way. | Narrated lesson video |
| Disengaged students | Slides read aloud lose the room. | a.i.-directed visuals, motion and transitions make old slides look produced. | Pro-quality direction |
| Revision and exams | Students ask for the same explanations before every test. | A library of short topic videos, reused each year. | Explain once, reuse forever |
| Inclusion (EAL/D, hearing, attention) | Text-heavy slides exclude some learners. | Captions plus narration; slower voice speeds; future multilingual narration for families. | Captions built in |
| Flipped and blended learning | Recording yourself is awkward and slow. | Teacher stays off-camera; the slides and narration do the work. | No camera needed |
| Parent communication | Parent nights and course info sessions are poorly attended. | Short explainer videos on assessment, subject selection and camps. | Shareable video |
| Tired old decks | Decks made years ago look dated. | Pro upgrades the visual design for impact without the teacher redesigning. | Deck enhancement |

Lead with time for teachers; lead with engagement and consistency for principals.

## Pricing plans for education

Four education tiers, all at Pro quality (a.i.-directed, no watermark), A$ ex GST, schools billed annually. Video allowances pooled at school level.

| Plan | For | Price (A$, ex GST) | Videos included | Extras |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher | One verified teacher (school email) | 19/month, or 190/school year | 8/month (or 100/year) | Top-ups at Pro rates; cancel anytime |
| Faculty Pack | 5 teachers in one department | 890/year | 500/year, pooled | Shared video library; one invoice |
| School Licence S / M / L | Whole school, by teaching staff: up to 30 / 31–80 / 81–150 | 2,400 / 4,900 / 7,900 per year | 1,200 / 3,000 / 5,500 per year, pooled | All teachers; admin dashboard; Google and Microsoft sign-in; invoice and PO; onboarding PD session |
| System Agreement | Department of education, diocese or school network | From 1,500 per school per year at 20+ schools; quoted | Pooled across the system | DPA, ST4S report, Australian hosting, central admin, usage reporting, named support |

**Why these numbers**

- Teacher at A$19 sits below Pro (A$29) and under what teachers pay out of pocket without approval. School-year price (A$190) is 2 months cheaper.
- School licences work out at roughly A$50–80 per teacher per year.
- System price per school drops with volume.
- Pooled allowances, not unlimited: each Pro video has real a.i. and voice costs. Top-up packs (e.g. 500 videos for A$490).

**Offers**

- School pilot: 6 weeks, up to 10 teachers, 100 videos, flat A$300 credited against the first-year licence.
- Founding Schools: first 20 schools lock in their price for 3 years.
- Champion reward: a teacher whose school buys a licence gets a year of Teacher plan credit.
- Students: included for supervised class projects only once student-data privacy work is done.

Basic (watermarked) stays the try-before-you-buy route. Never call it "free".

**Open question:** confirm real cost per Pro video (a.i. plus ElevenLabs). Allowances assume under about A$1 per video; if not, shrink allowances, not prices.
