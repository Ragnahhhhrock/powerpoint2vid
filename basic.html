Basic PowerPoint to video converter: questions

What is the difference between Basic and Pro?

Basic records your slides with narration, transitions and captions, and its exports carry a watermark. On Pro, a.i. directs the video: a tighter story, every slide redesigned as an animated scene, timed to the narration, with no watermark.

How do I convert a PowerPoint to a video on Basic?

Drop your .pptx into the converter above, choose a voice and video style, preview it, then export.

Can I convert Google Slides to video?

Yes. Download your deck from Google Slides as a PowerPoint (.pptx) file, then drop it in. The Google Slides tab gives you the export link.

Where does the narration come from?

From your speaker notes, or slide text if there are no notes.

What video format does Basic export?

Your browser records the video, usually as WebM (MP4 in browsers that support it). WebM plays in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and most video editors.

Will it look exactly like my PowerPoint?

Basic renders text, shapes, images and backgrounds. Complex effects such as SmartArt, charts, custom animations and embedded video are simplified or skipped. Pro rebuilds charts as animated charts.
